# MSA-IPTV

A native, privacy-first IPTV player for iOS, Android, Samsung TV and LG TV.

MSA-IPTV connects to your existing Xtream Codes or M3U provider and turns it
into a clean, fast, modern viewing experience for Live TV, Movies, and Series.
There are no accounts to create and no tracking — everything stays on your
device.

## Features

- **Live TV, Movies & Series** — browse by category, search across everything
- **Xtream Codes & M3U** playlist support
- **Continue Watching & Watch Again** — pick up right where you left off
- **Favorites** across all content types
- **Offline downloads** — save movies and episodes to watch without a connection
- **Rich metadata** — cast, crew, and artwork via TMDB
- **Adaptive interface** — sidebar navigation on iPad and Mac, tab navigation on iPhone
- **Privacy-first** — no analytics, no ads, no tracking; your credentials and
  viewing history never leave your device
