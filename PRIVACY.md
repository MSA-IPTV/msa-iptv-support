# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 19 July 2026**

MSA-IPTV ("the app", "we", "us") is a media player for Xtream Codes and M3U
IPTV services. This policy explains what data the app handles and how.

**In short: we do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to our
servers. We have no servers.**

## Data We Collect

**None.**

The app contains no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting, and no
tracking of any kind. We do not use the Advertising Identifier (IDFA), and the
app does not ask for tracking permission because it tracks nothing.

## Data Stored on Your Device

The following information is stored **only on your device** and is never
transmitted to us:

- **IPTV account credentials** (server address, username, password) that you
  enter to connect to your IPTV provider.
- **Your library and viewing state** — favorites, watch progress, continue-
  watching, and downloaded content.

This data lives in the app's local database on your device. Deleting the app
removes it. We have no access to it.

## Data Sent to Third Parties

The app communicates with the following third parties strictly to provide its
core functionality. None of these transmissions include your identity.

### 1. Your IPTV Provider
When you add a playlist, the app sends your username and password to **the
server address you entered** in order to authenticate and stream content. This
is your own IPTV provider — a service you have chosen and subscribed to. We are
not a party to this connection and never receive this data. Your provider's own
privacy policy governs how they handle it.

### 2. The Movie Database (TMDB)
To display cast, crew, and artwork, the app sends **content titles** (e.g. a
movie or series name) to [TMDB](https://www.themoviedb.org). These requests
contain no account credentials, no device identifiers, and nothing that
identifies you. This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified
by TMDB. TMDB's privacy policy is available at
https://www.themoviedb.org/privacy-policy.

## Children's Privacy

The app does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children.

## Network Security

IPTV servers frequently support only unencrypted (HTTP) connections. To remain
compatible with these providers, the app permits cleartext connect
server address you configure. Data you send to your provider may therefore
travel unencrypted, depending on your provider's configuration. Th
property of your IPTV provider, not of the app.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected
by the "Last updated" date above.
